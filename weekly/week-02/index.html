Week 2 Update

September 7 – September 11

Mathematics Inquiry

This week, we engaged in an active math inquiry exploring place value. Students worked with hands-on materials to break down two-digit numbers, deepening their conceptual understanding of tens, and units.

Building on these foundational counting patterns, we took our first steps into multiplication. We explored patterns foundational to multiplication concepts.

Language & Literacy

We kicked off our peer Buddy Reading program with Grade 1! Our Grade 2 students stepped into leadership roles, practicing expressive reading and guiding their younger buddies through shared stories.

We are also reviewing of the phonics sounds we learned last year. We are expanding our knowledge of phonics by identifying the gramaphones that make the sounds.To help reinforce these rules and build student ownership, the class created visual phonics posters that are now displayed around the room as ongoing learning anchors.

Science & Inquiry

In science, we began identifying what we already know about living things. Students completed an interactive animal sorting activity, grouping organisms by various self-selected characteristics and traits. This pre-assessment helps us gauge existing knowledge as we move deeper into our unit on living things and environments.
