Week 3 Update

September 14 – September 18

Mathematics Inquiry - 12 Blocks

This week we continued to develop our understanding of place value. The children enjoyed playing a game called '12 Blocks'. In this game students had to guess the number I chose. The number had to be able to be represented using 12 base-10 blocks. To help them guess the number we employed a 'code breaker' game element where students found out if digits were in the right place, in the number (but in the wrong place) or if they weren't in the number.

Language & Literacy

Every morning we read books. We are looking at a lot of different books. We are also practicing our sight words.

We continued our peer Buddy Reading program with Grade 1! Our Grade 2 students are continuing to do a great job guiding their younger buddies through shared stories.

We have been reading "Lulu and the Brontosaurs" in class for everyone.

We continued reviewing phonics sounds making posters for /t/ and /p/.

We started our writing journey. Students are gathering ideas to use in their writing at a latter date. This is a difficult process for some students and we are looking at expressing ourselves by drawing then using those drawings to make sentences.

Science & Inquiry

In science, we took a closer look at the skin covering of some animals. This led me realise that students were missing some knowledge and animal classification. We are learning about vertebrates and invertebrates and the different classes within these phyla.
